A beautiful and customizable photo frame with time and weather. ☀️

Turn your device into a personalized digital photo frame. It displays photos from your library, the current date and time, and live weather information.

Features

🖼️ Photo Frame

Displays photos from your selected album.

⚙️ Customizable Shuffle

Set the interval for how often photos change.

🌦️ Live Weather

Shows the current weather conditions and a 4-day forecast.

💡 Full-Screen Clock

Automatically displays a full-screen clock in low-light conditions.

🛡️ Pixel Shift

Periodically shifts UI elements to prevent screen burn-in.

💎 Premium Version

A one-time purchase to unlock the true "Always-On" mode.
