Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in the VistaDash app.

Information We Collect

The VistaDash app does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data. All information and photos processed by the app remain on your device.

Photo Library Access

The app requires your permission to access your photo library to display images within the app. This access is used solely for the app's core functionality of being a digital photo frame. Your photos are never sent to or stored on any external server or service. The app only reads the photos you have given it permission to access.

In-App Purchases

The app uses Apple's official in-app purchase system to unlock the premium version. We do not collect or have access to any of your payment information. All transactions are handled securely by the App Store.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at vistadash@crazyhungry.party.
